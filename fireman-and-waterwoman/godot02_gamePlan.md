## Game Plan (fill out on planning day)

**Game name:** Fireman and Waterwoman

**Platformer or RPG?** 

**Answer:** Platformer


**What is your game about?** Who is the player, and where are they?

**Answer:** The player is a man made of fire and the second player is a woman made of water. You work together to get crystals to become real people.


**What does the player do?** (Run and jump? Explore? Collect things? Avoid things?)

**Answer:** Run and jump over obstacles to collect crystals


**What are the controls?** 

**Answer:** WASD for fireman and Arrow keys for waterwoman


**How do you win?**

**Answer:** Collect all crystals in a level


**How do you lose?**

**Answer:** Not finish the level 


**What will things look like?** Mark at least 3 as **Piskel**. The rest can be **Found**.

| Thing | What it will look like | Piskel or Found? |
|---|---|---|
| Player | Found | Man on fire and woman made of water |
| Enemy | Piskel | Moving sponge, water and fire work as spikes |
| Pickup | Piskel | little gemstones that have a shining animation |
| Tiles / ground | Found | Dungeon style tile set |
| Other: | Piskel | Doors |

**Where will your sound come from?** (Brackeys sounds, found online, or made ourselves)

**Answer:** found online


**Draw or describe your first level or map.** What's at the start, the middle, and the end?

**Answer:** Fireman and waterwoman start on opposite sides, they climb up to the middle, collecting crytals along the way to the top. Fireman has to walk through lava to get to a button and waterwoman has to walk through water to get to a button. Once these are both pressed it opens up a barrier so they can go through the door.
